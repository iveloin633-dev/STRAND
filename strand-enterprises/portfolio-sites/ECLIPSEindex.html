What does creatine actually do?

It makes you stronger, and it shows. Within a few weeks your muscles look fuller and rounder, and you start getting an extra rep or two on your hardest sets. Stack those reps over months and you get the real thing: more strength, more power and more lean muscle. It's the most proven muscle-building supplement there is.

Is creatine a steroid or a drug?

Neither. It's a compound your body builds from three amino acids and gets from meat and fish. It doesn't act like a hormone, it's legal to buy, and it's allowed in every major sport.

Is it new?

Not even close. Creatine was discovered in meat in 1832 and has been sold as a supplement since the early 1990s. There are more than 500 published studies on it.

Is it safe?

For healthy people at these doses, yes. It's one of the most studied supplements there is, and trials haven't found kidney or liver harm in healthy users. Check with your doctor first if you have kidney disease, take medication, or are pregnant.

Can teenagers take it?

Yes. There's no federal age limit on creatine, and studies in teen athletes haven't found harm. The one exception is New York, which bans selling muscle-building supplements to anyone under 18, so we can't ship there to minors. Some pediatricians are more cautious than the research, so it's worth a quick word with a parent.

Is it a stimulant? Is it addictive?

No and no. Eclipse has zero caffeine and nothing that acts on your nervous system, so there are no jitters, no crash and nothing to get hooked on. If you ever stop, your muscle stores simply drift back to normal over a few weeks.

Will creatine make me bloated?

No. Creatine pulls water into the muscle, not under the skin, so you look fuller, not puffy.

Does creatine cause hair loss?

That worry traces back to one small 2009 study that saw a rise in DHT and never measured hair. A 2025 randomized trial checked both hormones and hair follicles for 12 weeks and found no difference from placebo.

Can I take Eclipse with the Pre-Workout?

Yes, they were designed together. The Pre-Workout has no beta-alanine, so nothing in the pair gets doubled.
