    Before You Come In

    Questions

    Do I need experience?

    No. Every new member gets a free intro session, and Foundations teaches the big lifts from zero. Most of our members had never touched a barbell before.

    What's in the free 3-day pass?

    Full gym access and up to three classes over three days. No card, no contract, no sales pitch at the end.

    Can I freeze or cancel?

    Starter is month to month. Forged and Elite can freeze up to two months a year, and you can cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

    How does 24/7 access work?

    Members get a keycard. Outside staffed hours the floor is camera-monitored and there's an emergency call button at every rack.

    What should I bring?

    Shoes you can lift in, a water bottle, and a towel. Chalk, belts and wraps are free to use.
