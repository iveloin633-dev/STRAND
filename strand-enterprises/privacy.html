Privacy

What happens to what you send.

Last updated October 22, 2026

Short version: we only collect what you type into the free preview form, we use it to reply to you, and we never sell it.

Who we are

Strand Enterprises builds websites for local businesses in Salt Lake City, Utah. Questions about this page go to strandsites@strandsites.onworkspace.com.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the free preview form:

  • Your business name
  • Your phone number or email
  • What you want the site to do

This site doesn’t use cookies, ad trackers, or analytics.

How we use it

To reply to you, build your free preview, and talk through your project. We won’t add you to a mailing list or send you marketing.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree receives the form and emails it to us. See Formspree’s privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this site. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the site. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

We don’t sell, rent, or share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to work with you. If you ask, we’ll delete it.

Your choices

Email strandsites@strandsites.onworkspace.com to see, correct, or delete anything you’ve sent us.

Changes
